# Blood Bridge: data sources

Blood Bridge contains no made-up facilities or statistics. Every blood bank, hospital, coordinate and calibration number comes from a public source and can be traced back to it. This page lists each source and its licence, explains how each dataset was cleaned, and states plainly which parts of the demo are simulated.

All sources were retrieved on **2026-11-20**. Any dataset can be rebuilt from its source:

```bash
python3 -I data/scripts/fetch_raw.py        # data.gov.in + OpenStreetMap  -> data/raw/
python3 -I data/scripts/fetch_eraktkosh.py  # live e-RaktKosh stock        -> data/raw/
python3 -I data/scripts/build_mumbai.py     # cleaned JSON                 -> data/mumbai/
```

## What is real vs simulated

| Real (sourced) | Simulated (generated by the app) |
|---|---|
| Names, categories, addresses, phone numbers and coordinates of 25 Mumbai blood centres | Individual blood units (bag IDs and per-bag expiry dates) |
| Names and coordinates of 6 municipal hospitals that act as requesters | Patients, clinical requests and reservations |
| A live stock snapshot: units per blood group and component for those 25 centres, from e-RaktKosh, fetched 2026-11-20 17:16 UTC | Donors and their donation history |
| ABO/Rh distribution, shelf lives, donor intervals, collection volumes | Transfers between sites, and how stock changes after the snapshot |
| Chronic flood-spot locations in Mumbai | The surge scenario itself (epicentre, casualty count, timeline) |
| Published blood-use rates in mass-casualty incidents | Surge multipliers for floods (**assumptions**, explained in `calibration.json` → `surgeAssumptions`) |

The snapshot gives aggregate counts only. To show expiry-aware transfers, the app splits those counts into individual units with simulated expiry dates, using the shelf lives in `calibration.json`.

## Datasets

### 1. `data/mumbai/blood_banks.json` (25 records)
- **Sources:**
  - **Directory record:** [data.gov.in Blood Bank Directory (updated till last month)](https://data.gov.in/resource/blood-bank-directory-updated-till-last-month), resource `fced6df9-a360-4e08-8ca0-f283fc74ce15`. Publisher: MoHFW / NIHFW (National Health Portal). The data.gov.in record was last updated 2022-07-12.
  - **Coordinates:** OpenStreetMap, via the Overpass API.
  - **Link to stock:** each bank is linked to its e-RaktKosh stock row.
- **Licences:**
  - data.gov.in: [GODL-India](https://data.gov.in/government-open-data-license-india).
  - OpenStreetMap: [ODbL 1.0](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), "© OpenStreetMap contributors".
- **Fields:**
  - `id`: stable slug, for example `bb-kem-parel`.
  - `name`, `address`: as written in data.gov.in.
  - `category`: `government`, `private`, `charitable` or `unknown`, mapped from the data.gov.in values Government, Private and Charity.
  - `area`, `phone`: `phone` is the first number listed in data.gov.in.
  - `lat`, `lng`, `source`.
  - `sourceId`: contains the data.gov.in `sr_no`, the OSM node and the exact e-RaktKosh name.
- **Cleaning:**
  1. Downloaded all 66 data.gov.in records for district MUMBAI and 31 for THANE. The district MUMBAI SUBURBAN returned no records.
  2. **Dropped records without usable coordinates:**
     - 10 Mumbai records have coordinates of `0,0`.
     - ACTREC (Kharghar) is outside the Mumbai bounding box, 18.85–19.30 N / 72.75–73.05 E.
     - "Hematology Laboratory Blood Bank" sits at 19.18 N (Malad) in data.gov.in, but its address is Mama Parmanand Marg in South Mumbai. The OSM position matches the address, so the data.gov.in coordinate is wrong.
  3. **Kept only banks that report stock on e-RaktKosh**, so that every site has real inventory, and trimmed the list to 25 spread across the city. Banks were linked by hand across the three sources because names changed: "Blood Bank" became "Blood Centre" under the 2020 Drugs Rules amendment.
  4. **Coordinates:** the OSM node is used when one exists, and data.gov.in otherwise. Note that many OSM blood-bank nodes (`source=OpenGovernmentData`) were imported from this same directory, so the two sources are not fully independent. For the 23 banks found in both sources, the largest difference is **84 m**, which is listed in `data/mumbai/_coordinate_check.json`.
  5. **Single-source coordinates:** Lilavati and Hiranandani (Powai) have coordinates from data.gov.in only. Lilavati is within about 30 m of the OSM hospital polygon. Hiranandani Powai has no OSM hospital to compare against.
- **Limitations:**
  - The directory dates from 2022, so phone numbers and categories may be out of date.
  - e-RaktKosh labels some government hospitals as "Charitable/Vol", for example Cooper. For the category field we use data.gov.in.

### 2. `data/mumbai/hospitals.json` (6 records)
- **Source and licence:** OpenStreetMap `amenity=hospital` inside the Mumbai bounding box (1,192 elements), ODbL.
- **Selected hospitals:** all are large MCGM (municipal) hospitals, near flood belts or trauma care:
  - LTMG Sion
  - Rajawadi (Ghatkopar)
  - V.N. Desai (Santacruz)
  - Shatabdi (Govandi)
  - HBT Trauma Care (Jogeshwari)
  - BYL Nair (Mumbai Central)
- **Fields:** the same shape as `blood_banks.json`, plus a `note` field. `name` is the OSM name, and `note` gives the official name.
- **Limitations:**
  - KEM Hospital is not tagged as a hospital in OSM, so it is included only as a blood bank.
  - Phone numbers are given only where OSM has them (Shatabdi). Otherwise `phone` is null.

### 3. `data/mumbai/stock_snapshot.json`: real live stock
- **Source:** e-RaktKosh, run by MoHFW / NBTC (National Blood Transfusion Council). Its public "Blood Availability" page ([stockAvailability.cnt](https://eraktkosh.mohfw.gov.in/BLDAHIMS/bloodbank/stockAvailability.cnt)) calls an **unauthenticated** JSON endpoint: `nearbyBB.cnt?hmode=GETNEARBYSTOCKDETAILS&stateCode=27&districtCode=519|518&bloodGroup=all&bloodComponent=<code>`.
- **What was fetched:** 14 components × 2 districts (Mumbai and Mumbai Suburban), 452 rows covering 52 blood centres.
- **When:** `fetchedAt` is 2026-11-20T17:16:07Z. Each centre's own last-update time, in IST, is in `bankLastUpdatedIST`.
- **Licence:** no explicit licence is published. This is public government information that e-RaktKosh shows to citizens. We use one snapshot and cite the source. No bulk scraping is done.
- **Shape:** `{ fetchedAt, source, unit, componentMapping, bankLastUpdatedIST, banks: { [bankId]: { [bloodGroup]: { whole_blood, red_cells, platelets, plasma } } } }`.
- **Component mapping:**
  - `red_cells`: PRBC, SAGM PRBC, leukoreduced RBC and irradiated RBC.
  - `platelets`: RDP, platelet concentrate and SDP.
  - `plasma`: FFP, plasma and single-donor plasma.
  - Left out: cryoprecipitate, cryo-poor plasma, platelet-rich plasma, and the Oh± (Bombay) phenotype.
- **Totals for the 25 banks:**
  - plasma: 8,303 units
  - red cells: 1,105 units
  - platelets: 349 units
  - whole blood: 4 units
  - **all components: 9,761 units**
- **Limitations:**
  - Counts are self-reported by each centre, and update frequency varies.
  - Groups that a centre does not list are recorded as 0. A zero can mean "none in stock" or "not reported".
  - Mumbai blood centres separate almost all collections into components, so whole blood is close to zero. The surplus of frozen plasma is also real.

### 4. `data/mumbai/calibration.json`
Every number in this file has a citation key that resolves to an entry in `citations[]`.

- **`bloodGroupShare`** (8 groups): derived from Agrawal et al. 2014 (*Asian J Transfus Sci*, n = 9,686 across a multicentre study). The inputs are the paper's **West-region (Mumbai) row**:
  - ABO: O 36.75%, B 32.74%, A 23.69%, AB 6.80%.
  - Rh(D): positive 92.97%, negative 7.02%.

  The paper reports ABO and Rh separately, so the eight shares are ABO × Rh, which assumes the two are independent. This is labelled in the file. The national figures from the same paper and a directly observed 8-group study (Sigamani 2022) are included for comparison.
- **`shelfLifeDays`:** Drugs Rules 1945, Schedule F Part XII-B, as amended by G.S.R. 166(E) in 2020:
  - whole blood: 35 days (CPDA-1)
  - red cells: 42 days (SAGM)
  - platelets: 5 days (20–24 °C with agitation)
  - FFP: 1 year (at or below −30 °C)
- **`donorIntervalDays`:** 90 days for males and 120 days for females (G.S.R. 166(E) 2020, which codifies the NBTC 2017 donor-selection guidelines). Age, weight and haemoglobin criteria are also included.
- **`annualCollection`:**
  - India 2024-25: 14,601,147 units (Rajya Sabha AU2995, as reported by Newslaundry).
  - India 2018: 12.4 M donations (WHO 2021).
  - Maharashtra 2025: about 23 lakh units, from SBTC data reported in the Free Press Journal.
  - Mumbai, Jan–May 2026: 1.27 lakh units (Free Press Journal).
  - Component separation in India: 53% of collections (Mammen et al. 2022).
- **`surgeEvidence` vs `surgeAssumptions`:**
  - **Evidence** comes from peer-reviewed mass-casualty data. Israeli terror-attack series report about 1 RBC unit per casualty for small events and 2 for large ones, with half the units used in the first 2 hours. Las Vegas 2017 used 2.7 components per admitted patient. The Israeli MoH says city-wide use "doubles or triples".
  - **No published blood-usage data for floods was found.** The flood multipliers are therefore clearly marked assumptions, with the reasoning written out in the file.

### 5. `data/mumbai/flood_zones.json` (7 records)
- **Localities:** chronic waterlogging spots that BMC and the press name again and again:
  - Hindmata
  - King's Circle / Gandhi Market (Sion)
  - Kurla (Mithi river)
  - Chunabhatti
  - Milan Subway
  - Andheri Subway
  - Masjid Bunder
- **Sources:** each record quotes and links its source (Citizen Matters 2024, Free Press Journal 2025/2026, Newslaundry 2021).
- **Coordinates:** OSM Nominatim (ODbL). The Andheri Subway point is approximate.
- **Context:** the 26 July 2005 flood brought 942 mm of rain in 24 h, according to NASA Earth Observatory. The Fact Finding Committee report gives 944.2 mm at Santacruz on 26–27 July. NASA counted 273 deaths in Mumbai as of 28 July.
- **Limitation:** we could not retrieve the official MCGM Disaster Management Plan PDF. The flood spots therefore rest on press reports that cite BMC.

## Sources that failed or are missing
- **Overpass API:** returned `406 Not Acceptable` to requests without a User-Agent. It worked once a descriptive User-Agent was sent.
- **data.gov.in:** works with the public sample API key, which returns 10 rows per page. The directory has no "MUMBAI SUBURBAN" district; suburban banks are listed under MUMBAI.
- **sansad.in / eparlib parliamentary PDFs:** timed out or refused the connection. National figures are cited through Newslaundry's report of the answer instead.
- **SBTC Maharashtra annual report:** not found online. The state's 2018–22 dataset on data.gov.in returned HTTP 403.
- **Missing data:** no Mumbai-specific peer-reviewed 8-group study, no blood-usage figures for 26/11 or the 2005 floods, and no data on platelet demand after floods.
